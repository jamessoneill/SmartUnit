# Code of Conduct

SmartUnit welcomes respectful contributors regardless of background, level of experience or employer. Be constructive and kind in discussions and code reviews. Assume good intent, ask for evidence, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person. Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory behaviour and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may moderate issues, discussions or pull requests that violate these expectations, including asking for edits, locking threads or restricting participation. To raise a conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately via the repository's available contact method; please don't share sensitive personal details in a public issue.
